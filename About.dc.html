Hi, I’m Sofia

I move between the hand and the screen: crochet, weaving and paint on one side, CAD, product models and editorial layout on the other. Most of my work starts with a material and ends with a question about who it’s for. I have special interests in material sustainability, prosthetics, and drug policy.
